ExaggerationAt this dialAt 1.0What it buys
Forgiveness widens the window, not the skill. The crack threshold, the equator advantage and the flat-versus-edge rule are untouched at every dial position. Only the upper bound moves — set Documentary and slam it, then Saturday Morning and slam it identically; the same impact goes from shattered to clean.
What this preview is not. Flat-shaded triangles standing in for the refraction-and-Fresnel material, an ellipsoid instead of the authored shell model, and a three-axis volume-preserving sac instead of the 160-node membrane lattice — so the yolk squashes, rings and tears, but cannot split along a seam. The field solver, the yield stress, the cohesion rim and the crack test are the shipped ones, ported line for line. The mound also stands taller here than a real one does: the yield stress acts as an angle of repose, and that is the next tuning question rather than something the preview hides.
