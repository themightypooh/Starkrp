ExaggerationAt this dialAt 1.0What it buys
Forgiveness widens the window, not the skill. The crack threshold, the equator advantage and the flat-versus-edge rule are untouched at every dial position. Only the upper bound moves — drag the dial to Saturday Morning and the same slam that shattered the egg at Documentary opens it cleanly.
What this preview is not. Flat shading standing in for the refraction and Fresnel material; the shell is an ellipsoid rather than the authored model; and the yolk is a three-axis volume-preserving sac standing in for the 160-node membrane lattice, so it squashes and rings correctly but cannot tear along a seam. The field solver, the yield stress, the cohesion rim and the crack test are the shipped ones, ported line for line.
