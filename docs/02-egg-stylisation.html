ExaggerationAt this dialAt 1.0What it buys
Forgiveness widens the window, not the skill. The crack threshold, the equator advantage and the flat-versus-edge rule are untouched at every dial position. Only the upper bound moves, because losing a breakfast to a physically correct 4in/s overswing is not a mechanic — it is a bug with a rationale.
What this preview is not. Two-dimensional lighting standing in for the refraction-and-Fresnel material, no shell, no pour particles, and the section is drawn at the renderer's vertical exaggeration rather than the shader's. The rheology, the yield stress, the cohesion rim and the membrane springs are the real ones. Treat the colours as indicative and the shapes as load-bearing.
