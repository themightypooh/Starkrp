7 · Before this locks

01

Vault, or flat stainless fascia?

The crown only reads from the ends and in three-quarter view — head-on you just see the 128 eave. A flat fascia band hiding the vault is equally authentic to the type and much cheaper to build. Vault wins on silhouette; fascia wins as a sign surface.

02

Which end takes the kitchen?

Currently east. If players approach the lot from the east, flip it — arrivals should meet the dining car, not the service block and its bins.

03

The lot.

Parking assumed west and rear. No site geometry is committed — and on a main street, R9 makes the neighbours' frontage line matter more than anything on this plot.
